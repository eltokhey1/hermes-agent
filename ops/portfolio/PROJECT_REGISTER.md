## Evidence and profitability discipline
Never treat seller-advertised ARR/MRR, a current listing price, payment-connected platform data, or revenue from a previous chat as audited **net profit**. Require exact dated source, metric definition, currency, expenses, customer retention and acquisition costs. A technical build estimate is a *scenario*, not a vendor quote.

## Commercial gates for the three original product tracks
- **TradeDocs AI first:** ten targeted interviews and synthetic document extraction/diff tests; demonstrate measured time-saving and one concrete paid pilot commitment.
- **ExportLead AI second:** one vertical/region, licensed prospect data, no unsolicited sending, clear measurable lead quality and conversion.
- **Shipment Intelligence third:** check licensed carrier feeds, event provenance and freshness, ETA uncertainty, no container status inferred solely from vessel position.

All three require an independently approved security/data-processing boundary before real customer documents or external effects.
