# TradeDocs AI: global scope and four-language UI

Supported **interface vocabulary**: Arabic (ar, RTL), English (en), French (fr), Spanish (es). The initial dictionary covers 14 labels; this is **not** a fully translated product, nor does it translate source trade documents or regulatory texts. Country identifiers should use ISO 3166-1 alpha-2, currencies ISO 4217, and products locally reviewed HS classifications (edition and jurisdiction required).

Global route model: origin country, export country, transit countries, import country, HS code, product, shipment date, destination port, Incoterm edition, bank/LC terms. Never limit the platform to Libya. **Country rule coverage is currently zero verified country packs**; show UNKNOWN/NEEDS_REVIEW for jurisdictions without sourced, current rules, rather than invent requirements.

Country packs must include authoritative source URL, effective date, publication date, commodity/HS scope, import/export direction, licensing, original/copy and legalization conditions, exceptions, independent review and refresh cadence. Regional blocs and sanctions/export controls may apply in addition to national rules and require their own sourced modules. No actual official requirements have been fetched or certified here.

Do not publish bank data, enable cloud writes, or merge/deploy without HITL approvals. PRODUCTION=HOLD.
